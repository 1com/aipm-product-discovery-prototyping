# 03 – Group Exercise: Multi-Page Prototype Challenge

## 💡 Why this exercise matters
This collaborative exercise puts all your individual learning into practice.  
It simulates a real product sprint — combining teamwork, branding, and user flow design.

## 🎯 Expected outcomes
- A 3-page, branded prototype that feels like a real product  
- Clear division of roles (copy, UX, visual design)  
- Experience in collaborative GenAI-driven design

---

## 🧭 Team Challenge – Professional Multi-Page Prototype
**Build Groups of 3–4 people**

**Your Mission:**  
Create a **3-page prototype** with custom branding that feels like a real product.

**Time:** 20 minutes

---

## 🧩 Step 1: Build 3-Page Prototype
Copy, fill in, and paste this prompt into Lovable.dev:

Create a professional workshop sales website for "[WORKSHOP TITLE, e.g. 'GenAI for Product Teams']" - 
a training program that helps [TARGET AUDIENCE, e.g. "product managers and UX designers"] 
to [MAIN OUTCOME, e.g. "leverage AI tools in their daily workflow"].

BUILD THESE 3 CORE PAGES:

1. LANDING PAGE:
- Compelling hero section with workshop title and value proposition 
- "What you'll learn" section with 4-5 key takeaways 
- Social proof: testimonials, logos, or success stats 
- Trainer bio with photo and credibility indicators 
- Prominent "Book Now" or "Learn More" buttons 
- FAQ section addressing common concerns 

2. WORKSHOP DETAILS PAGE:
- Detailed agenda with time blocks 
- Learning objectives and outcomes 
- "Who should attend" description 
- Prerequisites and preparation 
- Workshop format and pricing info 

3. BOOKING PAGE:
- Schedule with available dates 
- Ticket options (early bird, group discounts) 
- Registration form and payment details 
- Confirmation and contact info 

---

## 🎨 Step 2: Add Custom Branding
After your prototype generates, paste this prompt:

Transform this into a premium workshop experience using my existing brand identity:

APPLY MY BRAND SYSTEM:
- Use my brand colors: [Primary: #EFCB68] [Secondary: #E1EFE6] [Accent: #yourcolor]
- Apply my typography: [Primary font] for headings, [Secondary font] for body text
- Integrate my existing logo and visual identity elements
- Match my brand personality: [professional/creative/innovative/approachable]

CONVERSION PSYCHOLOGY UPGRADES:
- Add scarcity indicators ("Only 12 seats left")
- Include social proof and success outcomes
- Add risk-reversal elements (refund policy)
- Include urgency (early bird pricing, limited seats)

AUTHORITY & CREDIBILITY BOOSTERS:
- Showcase achievements, case studies, and logos
- Include participant testimonials and certifications

PREMIUM USER EXPERIENCE:
- Add micro-animations and hover effects
- Interactive FAQ, progress bars, and pricing tables
- Use professional imagery and smooth booking flow

TRUST & SECURITY ELEMENTS:
- Display security badges and payment protection
- Include refund policy, terms, and privacy links

---

## 🧠 Outcome
A **premium, branded, multi-page prototype** that demonstrates professional UX, credible branding, and conversion-focused design — ready for presentation or early user testing.
